Products

Instruments and software that have been publicly announced.

This page keeps to what was presented in public: two Qiuhao AI microscopes, and a self-developed fishery management platform. Prices, unstated optical specifications, and overseas product promises are not listed here.

Qiuhao AI microscopes

Launched in Guangzhou on 10 September 2026.

Qiuhao 5 and Qiuhao 8 were introduced at the 12th Guangzhou International Fishery Expo. They are presented as tools for aquatic disease microscopy: a user examines a sample, and the system is described as supporting identification and a suggested course of action. Deputy General Manager Lei Lei spoke at the launch. Domestic dealer recruitment was announced at the same time.

Announced positioning

Qiuhao 8

Described for research institutes, large farms, and professional testing laboratories — settings that need a higher standard of microscopic imaging.

Announced positioning

Qiuhao 5

Described for smaller farms, grassroots aquatic pharmacies, and front-line practitioners.

A disease-recognition WeChat mini-program has been offered free of charge in China, including for users who do not purchase a microscope. That offering is a China-channel service. It is not presented on this site as a product promise for overseas buyers.

Figures stated by the company

Company-stated identification figures, not independently certified results.

A group party-committee article described the underlying identification work in the following terms: more than 70,000 disease-sample images, more than 140,000 annotation boxes, and 71 common parasites and algae identifiable, with stated accuracy bands of more than 90 percent for 20 types, more than 80 percent for 24 types, and more than 70 percent for 18 types. The same article said devices were already in use at some aquatic hospitals and fish-disease testing centers, and that the equipment had completed design finalization and was entering launch preparation — a statement made before, or around, the September 2026 Expo launch.

Those numbers come from a company-affiliated article. They have not been independently certified in the public record used to prepare this site, and they should be read that way.

How to read this page

No CE marks or other international product certifications are shown, because none have been publicly verified for these instruments. Official photographs of Qiuhao 5 and Qiuhao 8 will be added when the company can supply images it has the right to use.

Zhihui Fishery Management Platform

A way to watch the workshop as a single record.

The same public article describes a self-developed fishery management platform used in the workshop. The modules named there are listed below. This is a description of announced functions, not a software specification sheet.

Water-quality monitoring

Continuous attention to the indicators that keep a recirculating system stable.

Production logs

A written record of culture work, so activity can be reviewed later.

Equipment control, alerts, and analysis

Named together with data analysis as part of the same platform.
