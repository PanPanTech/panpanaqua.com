Services

Start with a visit. Then talk about support that can actually be given.

The useful first step is to see the Nansha workshop. What follows is the kind of service conversation we can hold honestly — not a promise of a worldwide duty engineer on every site.

Visit the base

Arranged visits to the recirculating workshop, for farms, researchers, and organizations that want to see the practice in place before talking about instruments or methods.

Operational conversation

Discussion of water-quality management, workshop routines, pond-relay practice, and how the management platform is used on site.

Training

Training can be discussed around the workshop, the platform, and Qiuhao instruments — in Nansha first, and in other arrangements only where they can be staffed and planned.

After a visit

Spare parts and remote support, as a model — not a global service network.

Serious use of instruments and of a recirculating workshop depends on spare parts, commissioning help, and a way to ask for advice when something is unstable. We are prepared to discuss those needs and to say clearly what can be provided from Nansha, and what cannot yet be promised at a distance.

This site does not publish service-level times, regional offices, or a map of overseas technicians, because no such network has been documented in the public record used here.

Where interest may grow

Water-scarce and warm-water regions.

Recirculating methods are often discussed where water is scarce, or where warm-water species are already farmed. We are interested in serious conversations in those settings, including Gulf countries and Southeast Asian warm-water markets.

Interest is not the same as a completed project. This page does not claim existing work in those regions.
