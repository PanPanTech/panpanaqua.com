Recirculating aquaculture · Nansha, China

Recirculating aquaculture, practiced in the Greater Bay Area.

PanPan Aqua is the public site of Guangzhou Nansha Zhihui Agricultural Technology Co., Ltd. The work centers on a visitable recirculating aquaculture workshop in Nansha, a fishery management platform, and Qiuhao AI microscopes for aquatic-health detection.

A note on scale

Bay Area Agri Valley is described in a Guangzhou environmental impact approval (Yue Nan Shenpi EIA [2026] No. 10, 2 February 2026) as intelligent fish-culture experiments and observational research, with a designed annual experimental culture volume of about 100,000 jin — roughly 50 tonnes. The project’s published total investment of about RMB 853 million should not be read as the capacity of a large commercial fish factory.

What you will find here

Three parts of the work, plainly stated.

A demonstration workshop

A Phase 1 aquaponics intelligent-factory workshop is already in operation in Nansha. Visitors can see recirculating culture practice, water-quality work, and pond-relay farming in its local setting.

RAS operations and the base

A fishery management platform

The company has described a self-developed platform that brings together water-quality monitoring, production logs, equipment control, alerts, and data analysis.

Platform and products

Qiuhao AI microscopes

Qiuhao 5 and Qiuhao 8 were launched on 10 September 2026 at the 12th Guangzhou International Fishery Expo, for aquatic disease microscopy and identification support.

How we describe the company

An operator of RAS practice in Nansha — not a global turnkey contractor.

Public descriptions of the business include factory RAS operations, pond-relay aquaculture, and the development and operation of a modern agricultural park, including Bay Area Agri Valley. We keep to that record.

This site does not claim overseas delivery contracts, a worldwide engineering service network, or a large commercial land-based salmon factory, because those claims are not supported by the public record used to build these pages.
